- **The master volume is raised for an alarm's critical announcement, not for a condition that holds, with a *Raise Volume* switch per alarm (`lib/audio.lua`, `app/pages/settings/audio/events/category_page.lua`, `lib/preferences.lua`, `i18n/en.lua`, `i18n/de.lua`, `docs/audio/events.md`, `docs/pages/settings/audio/volume.md`, `bin/accounting/budgets.lua`)**:
  - In the *Master volume* mode of *Settings* > *Audio* > *Volume*, the *Alert Level* is written right before an alarm's critical announcement plays, and the *Normal Level* 8 seconds after the last one; another critical announcement within that time extends it without a write. Critical is the upper level of an alarm with two levels, and the only level of main power lost, the BEC / receiver alert, telemetry lost, telemetry dropouts and the empty SmartFuel alarm.
  - The low-voltage alert gains a critical level at the flight controller's minimum cell voltage times the cell count, held for *Hold* as the warning is. It is entered as the temperature and link quality levels are: the same announcement is made at once and its *Repeat* count starts again, in every mode -- with the voltage alert on, the default, a pack that goes on from the warning threshold to the minimum is announced once more than before. No new sound file. The level stays until the alert clears. Before it is reached the volume is not raised.
  - Before, the volume followed a condition: the low-voltage alert raised it from the first reading under the warning threshold, before *Hold*, so a sag that never got spoken wrote the global variable twice; a telemetry loss held it raised for 120 seconds; a temperature for as long as it stayed critical. None of these raises it any more unless the alarm is spoken.
  - A *Raise Volume* switch under each alarm on its *Events* page, shown while that alarm is on, 15 switches for 16 alarms (the BEC and receiver alert share one, on the *Voltage* page), on by default except *Telemetry Dropouts*. They act only in *Master volume*, and vibration stays as the page sets it.
  - With *Connected only* on, the link going down now writes `-1024` at once also while the volume is raised.
  - A settings file in *Off* or *Callout level*, the default, hears nothing different; the switches are written to the file on the first save of their page.
  - The *Voltage* page draws two more rows with its alert on, the default, so its build budget is raised from 6100 to 7950 (`bin/accounting/budgets.lua`, the measured 6335 times 1.25).
