- **Tune Advisor follows polar cyclic rates (`app/pages/flight_tuning/tune_advisor/page.lua`,
  `docs/pages/flight_tuning/tune_advisor.md`)**:
  - With polar cyclic rates, Roll and Pitch rate suggestions name the Cyclic row and work from the Pitch rates
    the flight controller uses for the whole cyclic stick. The page heading shows the rate profile the advice is for
    and follows a profile switch.
  - The documentation explains when the statistics reset and how to get a clean measurement.
