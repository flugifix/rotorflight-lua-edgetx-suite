- **Quick menu on a fixed two-column raster, battery profile on a page of its own (`widgets/dashboard/fullscreen_menu.lua`, `widgets/dashboard/battery_profile_menu.lua`, `widgets/dashboard/quick_menu_order.lua`, `widgets/dashboard/views.lua`, `widgets/dashboard/themes/urban/`, `widgets/dashboard/themes/grid/`, `widgets/dashboard/themes/deck/`, `app/pages/settings/dashboard/quick_menu/help.lua`, `i18n/en.lua`, `i18n/de.lua`, `bin/themes/validate.lua`, `docs/dashboard/quick-menu.md`, `docs/pages/settings/dashboard/quick_menu.md`, `docs/developer/dashboard-views.md`, `docs/developer/dashboard-themes.md`)**:
  - The fullscreen quick menu lays the pilot's entries on six places of one size, two columns from a 300 px wide zone and one below that, filled row by row and left to right. A hidden entry takes no place, and the buttons keep their size whatever is shown.
  - BATTERY PROFILE is no longer one of the *Quick Settings* positions (five now). The menu draws it as its own full-width row under the others, while the model is disarmed and the flight controller has reported a profile with a capacity, with the profile in force on the right (`6S · 2000mAh · Warn 3.50V`, shortened to fit; the capacity alone on firmware without per-profile cell values).
  - The row opens a battery profile page: one button per profile, with the cell count and warning voltage under the capacity where reported. A press sets and saves the profile and the page stays open, showing *SENDING...*, *SET AND SAVED* or *NOT SAVED*; while armed the profiles cannot be pressed and the page says so. Urban, Grid and Deck draw the page in their own look, and their *Profile & Tuning* buttons carry the same second line.
  - A stored *Quick Settings* list that names the battery profile reads without it; no migration.
