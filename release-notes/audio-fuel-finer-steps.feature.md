- **SmartFuel callouts can step finer near the end, and a step is no longer lost to the gap after another announcement (`lib/audio.lua`, `app/pages/settings/audio/events/category_page.lua`, `docs/audio/events.md`)**:
  - *Settings > Audio > Events > SmartFuel* has two new rows under *Callout %*. *Finer below* (Off, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 40 or 50 %) names a level from which the callout speaks every *Finer step* (1, 2 or 5 %): the *Callout %* steps above it, the level itself, then every multiple of the step below it. Off by default, which keeps the callouts as they were (#735).
  - A SmartFuel step whose announcement was refused by the quarter-second gap after the previous file is now spoken on the next pass instead of being marked as spoken; a sound pack without the callout file still marks the step once. *Main Power Back* is announced the same way rather than lost (#599).
