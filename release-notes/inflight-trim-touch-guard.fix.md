- **In-flight tuning: trim and touch refuse each other's step (`widgets/dashboard/inflight/drive.lua`, `docs/dashboard/inflight-tuning.md`)**:
  - A tap on a step control during a trim's pulse, or the pause after it, is refused with *too fast - one step at a time* instead of replacing the trim's value before the flight controller has counted it, or stretching it into a second step. A trim press during a touch pulse, a hold or the pause after them is refused with the same line instead of being lost, or stepping late once the touch is done.
  - A walk or bank trim pressed while a step is still on the wire shows the same line instead of being dropped without a word.
