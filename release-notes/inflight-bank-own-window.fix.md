- **In-flight tuning, Custom layout: a bank whose parked value would enable another window is refused (`widgets/dashboard/inflight/prime.lua`, `docs/dashboard/inflight-tuning.md`)**:
  - Each bank is parked at the middle of its enable window, held inside the channel's reach of 988 to 2012 µs. A window starting above 2012 µs, or two overlapping windows, put that value inside another slot's enable window, so a step went to the other window's parameter while the screen named the selected bank's. The derivation now holds every parked value against every enable window on the bank channel; a bank that does not enable its own window alone stays empty, the trims step over it, and its slots are counted as skipped (#585, site 1).
