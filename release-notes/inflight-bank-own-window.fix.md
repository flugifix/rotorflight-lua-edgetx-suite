- **In-flight tuning, Custom layout: a bank whose parked value would enable another slot is refused (`widgets/dashboard/inflight/prime.lua`, `docs/dashboard/inflight-tuning.md`)**:
  - Each bank is parked at the middle of its enable window, held inside the channel's reach of 988 to 2012 µs. A window beyond that reach, or two overlapping windows, could put that value inside another slot's enable window, so a step went to the other slot's parameter while the screen named the selected bank's. The derivation now holds every parked value against the enable window of every slot whose enable and value channels are the overlay's two, a continuous slot's included; a bank that does not enable its own window alone gets no assigned rows, nothing steps in it, the walk and the bank trim step over it, and its slots are counted as skipped (#585).
