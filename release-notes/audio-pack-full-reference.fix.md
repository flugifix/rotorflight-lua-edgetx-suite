- **Pack Not Full judges a pack against Full cell voltage, and Model Type says what AUTO decides (`lib/audio.lua`, `app/pages/settings/audio/events/category_page.lua`, `lib/preferences.lua`, `app/pages/setup/power/preferences/help.lua`, `i18n/en.lua`, `i18n/de.lua`, `docs/audio/events.md`, `docs/dashboard/urban-theme.md`, `docs/developer/dashboard-themes.md`, `docs/pages/setup/power/preferences.md`)**:
  - The *Pack Not Full* announcement and the not-full verdict a dashboard theme draws compare the pack's voltage per cell with the flight controller's *Full cell voltage* (default 4.10 V) instead of its *Max cell voltage* (default 4.30 V, the top of a cell's range, used to detect the cell count). On the defaults a freshly charged pack was announced as not full and drawn orange; it now counts as full from 4.10 V per cell. On firmware with cell voltages per battery profile it is the active profile's (#739).
  - The *Margin* defaults to 0 mV, the flight controller's own rule for its "battery not full" warning, and its range starts at 0 instead of 10. A settings file that already holds a margin keeps it: 100 mV, the former default, puts the threshold at 4.00 V per cell on the defaults.
  - *Model Type*'s help text and page say what the setting does: it picks the words of the SmartFuel announcements (battery or fuel) and does not change the fuel estimate, and *AUTO* counts the machine as electric when the flight controller has a cell count or a capacity set. An electric helicopter left at Rotorflight's defaults (both 0) needs *ELECTRIC*. No behaviour changes (#714).
