- Helicopter Setup lists PWM, DSHOT150 and DSHOT300 first and every other ESC protocol under Other, since DSHOT600 is sensitive to noise on long ESC leads. DSHOT150 sends no bidirectional DShot RPM: choosing it turns bidirectional DShot off in the same motor setting, the motor card says the governor and RPM filter need DSHOT300 or an RPM sensor, and the RPM checks no longer count it as a source. The tail is calibrated at 22.5° each way, where the two tail blades seen end-on open to 45°, checked with a square of paper folded corner to corner (`helicopter_setup`).
