- **FlyRotor and YGE read the ESC back after a save (`app/pages/setup/esc_motors/esc_tools/escmfg/flrtr/page.lua`, `app/pages/setup/esc_motors/esc_tools/escmfg/yge/page.lua`, `docs/pages/setup/esc_motors/esc_tools/escmfg/flrtr.md`, `docs/pages/setup/esc_motors/esc_tools/escmfg/yge.md`, `docs/reference/saving.md`)**:
  - After a save the page reads the parameters again, as the Hobbywing V5 and Scorpion pages do, waiting while the flight controller refreshes its copy of the ESC's block. The page then shows what the ESC reports rather than what was typed, Save is held until that read has answered instead of a quick second save failing as *ESC did not respond*, and on YGE a saved *Current Limit* is remembered for the model at once rather than at the next visit (#849).
  - A read that answers after the page was left changes nothing on it.
  - Every setting on both pages has its own `?` (English and German), the FlyRotor page `?` is one sentence about the page, and the YGE header `?`, which was drawn disabled, opens the page help (`app/pages/setup/esc_motors/esc_tools/escmfg/flrtr/help.lua`).
  - The instruction accounting (`bin/accounting/measure.lua`, `bin/accounting/budgets.lua`) now opens both pages, with the ESC protocol and parameter reply each needs and a card of their own, so the YGE page's stored current limit cannot reach the pages measured after it.
