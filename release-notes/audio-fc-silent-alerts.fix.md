- **Alerts on a flight controller reading wait while its telemetry is silent, and an alert whose sound file is missing is no longer tried on every pass (`lib/audio.lua`, `docs/audio/events.md`)**:
  - With the radio link up and the flight controller's telemetry frames away for 6 seconds -- the test *Telemetry Lost* is spoken on -- the radio goes on reporting the last values. Low pack voltage, *Main Power Lost*, the BEC / receiver alert, ESC and MCU temperature, ESC load, the empty SmartFuel alarm, the SmartFuel callouts and the SmartFuel level on connect now wait until a frame arrives instead of repeating that reading every ten seconds, and a critical one no longer raises the volume for it. Nothing they have counted starts over, and a callout that fell into the silence is spoken when the frames are back. The link alerts, the flight timer and the announcements made on a change are judged as before (#744).
  - An alert whose file the sound pack lacks counts the announcement as made, so it is tried again after its ten seconds rather than on every audio pass, spends its *Repeat* count, and does not vibrate; the log names each missing file once instead of two warnings per pass. An announcement refused by the quarter-second gap after the previous file is still tried on the next pass (#475).
