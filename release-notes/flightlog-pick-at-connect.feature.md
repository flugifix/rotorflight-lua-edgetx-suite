- **Flight log: the pack is chosen on the dashboard, and the dashboard asks for it whenever the log is on (`app/pages/tools/flight_log/page.lua`, `app/pages/tools/flight_log/help.lua`, `lib/battery_pick.lua`, `lib/preferences.lua`, `widgets/dashboard/battery_pick_menu.lua`, `docs/pages/tools/flight_log.md`, `docs/dashboard/quick-menu.md`, `docs/developer/dashboard-themes.md`)**: The *Battery for the next flight* row leaves the Flight Log page's *Batteries* tab, and the *Ask which pack after connecting* setting is gone. While *Write a flight log* is on, the dashboard widget asks which pack is on the craft after connecting, wherever the registry has a pack for that model; *BATTERY* in the quick menu brings the picker back as before. Renaming or deleting a pack on the page still takes the model's stored choice with it. A card that still carries the old setting has it ignored, and the key leaves the file at the next save of the settings, without a message.
